Hello! I’m Shania Dhea Bahana, a BSIT student who began my academic journey as a HUMSS student. Coming from HUMSS helped me build my confidence in communication, creativity, critical thinking, and working with others.

When I transitioned to Information Technology, I discovered a new interest in technology and the different ways it can be used to solve problems and make things easier.
